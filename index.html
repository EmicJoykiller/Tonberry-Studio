GOOD TO KNOW

FAQs

Clear answers before you get started.

What kind of photos should I send?+

A few clear photos from different angles are a great start. Add any outfit, accessory, or pose reference that helps tell the story. You can share them after we reply to your first note.

Can I make one of my pet or my whole family?+

Absolutely. Tell us who you’d like in the scene and what details matter most. We’ll talk through what will work best as a finished figure.

How much does a custom figure cost?+

Each idea is a little different. Send us the people, size, and style you have in mind and we’ll come back with a quote before anything is made.

Do I need a finished idea before I reach out?+

Nope. “I want to make something for my dad and his dog” is more than enough to begin. We can figure out the rest together.
